# attribution_cost_ratios_to_M0.csv

Feed-cost ratios from the matched-pool (attribution) experiment, restated against the nominal least-cost ration M0 of each case. This puts them on the same baseline as the LP re-solve control. Scope: TAB world (SD-H0), C0, cases A (dev_case_v3a) and C (dev_case_v3c), replicates 0-2 (roots r0-r2). Policies: Q0-Q4 x {static, delta}, plus the two SLP reference rows. That gives 72 rows. Feed cost is the engine definition `cost = p @ q` (docs/ENGINE_API.md): `q` is kg as-fed per head per day, and `p` is the fixed as-fed price per kg, which is not redrawn per test state. For a delta policy, cost is the mean over the 20,000 test states of the replicate. `cost_ratio_to_Q0_static` is the published `feed_cost_ratio_to_Q0_static` (R6_attribution_risks.csv). `Q0_static_to_M0` = cost(Q0_static ration of that replicate) / cost(M0). `cost_ratio_to_M0` = `cost_ratio_to_Q0_static` x `Q0_static_to_M0`. The last column is computed from unrounded values and then rounded to 4 decimals, so the product of the two rounded columns can differ from it in the fourth decimal. Three-decimal figures in the article were rounded once from the unrounded ratios; rounding the four-decimal values of this file a second time can differ by one unit in the third decimal (a value printed here as 1.1235 that lies just below 1.12350 appears as 1.123 in the article). M0 is the engine M0_nominal solution of the MAIN9 problem, the ration used by the LP re-solve control. It is one deterministic ration per case, and it is the same ration in A and C: cottonseed, the extra ingredient in C, is not used, and the shared prices are identical. `Q0_static_to_M0` varies by replicate only because the training-selected Q0 static ration depends on the root. In A, r0 and r2 select the same ration. In C, all three roots select the same ration.

Cross-checks (relative discrepancies; all at floating-point round-off unless stated):
- Prices. The as-fed price vector taken from the dev-case problem files (USD/lb divided by 0.45359237, then multiplied by the decision-time DM fraction for DM-basis quotes) equals the vector stored by the LP re-solve control exactly. Those prices reproduce the stored cost of every pool ration in every attribution cell (599 per cell in A, 978 per cell in C) with discrepancy 0.
- Policy costs. In all six cells, the mean cost of every policy was recomputed from the frozen ration vectors, the per-state assignments and the prices, then divided by the recomputed Q0 static cost. This covers Q0-Q4 static and delta, plus both SLP rows. Every result agrees with the published ratio to at most 5e-16. The cell summaries agree with the published ratios to at most 3e-16.
- M0. M0 cost is equal across three sources: the LP re-solve control record (identical in its three seeds), `p @ q` of that record's M0 ration, and the M0_nominal rows of the official v3 runs (75 rows per case, one distinct ration). The cost discrepancy is 0 and the ration discrepancy at most 2e-15 of total as-fed mass.
- Identity. The attribution cells and the control share the same ingredient axis, world fingerprint and reference-specification fingerprint.

The attribution test states and the control's test states are different draws from the same world. Fixed rations, including M0, cost the same in every state. The delta and re-solve means are each averaged over their own 20,000 states.
